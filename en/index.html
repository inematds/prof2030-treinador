AI Trainer: teach teams to delegate, review and approve work done with AI

For people who will run workshops and bootcamps and train teams and leaders using INEMA's open content. You learn how to teach adults, diagnose the class, build the workshop, train the right habit, teach safety with real cases and assess who learned.

Start with lesson 1 See Professional 2030

  • 1workshop kit at the end

The lessons

Each lesson ends with one piece of your kit. In lesson 8 you put it all together for the Horizonte Distribuidora customer service team.

  1. Lesson 1 · 18 minTeaching adults: the 5 principles that make the class use it on MondayAdults don't learn AI by sitting through a lecture. They learn when they see the why, work on a problem of their own, practice with feedback, try to remember without looking, and use it again the following week. This lesson turns that into 5 principles and a lesson plan you can reuse in every workshop.
  2. Lesson 2 · 17 minAI literacy as a product: diagnose the class and propose the trainingTraining that works for everyone works for no one. Before you design the workshop, find out what level the class is at, who already uses AI and who is afraid of it. With that in hand, your proposal to the owner stops being "an AI course" and becomes AI literacy with a goal and a measure.
  3. Lesson 3 · 22 min1-hour workshop, 4-hour workshop and bootcamp: the same 5-part structureThe format changes, the backbone doesn't. A 1-hour workshop, a 4-hour morning and a 5-session bootcamp follow the same 5 parts: destination, route, flight plan, in-flight service and landing. In this lesson you build the 1-hour script and practice the hardest part: the skeptical participant.
  4. Lesson 4 · 20 minDelegate, review, approve: train the habit instead of the "magic prompt"2026 tools deliver documents, spreadsheets, and even act on their own for hours. Memorizing prompts has lost value; what the team needs is a habit: ask well, check with judgment, and know what only goes out with someone's approval. This lesson brings the cycle and three exercises with answer keys to use in any class.
  5. Lesson 5 · 18 minAdapt to the learner's area: sales, customer service, HR, legal, finance and operationsA salesperson doesn't want a lawyer's example, and finance doesn't want a marketing example. The same cycle of delegate, review and approve gets stronger when it uses the task, the vocabulary and the risk of each area. This lesson gives you the adaptation method and a map of INEMA's open courses by area, so you can build the track for each class.
  6. Lesson 6 · 18 minSecurity as a lesson: teach risk with real cases, without scaring peopleA list of prohibitions doesn't change behavior, and fear paralyzes the class. A real case, told in 2 minutes, tied to something that exists in the company and closed with a simple rule, does change it. This lesson brings the 10-minute incident mini-lesson and three recent cases with sources for you to use.
  7. Lesson 7 · 20 minAssessing the class: rubric, practical test, internal champions and follow-up"I really liked it" on a satisfaction survey doesn't prove anyone learned. You need to see the person doing the work, against a clear standard, and come back weeks later to see whether the habit stuck. This lesson covers the 4-level rubric, the practical test, how to choose the internal champion, and the 30-, 60- and 90-day follow-up plan.
  8. Lesson 8 · 25 minWorkshop kit for Horizonte and what changed in the marketTime to put the pieces together. The final project is a 1-hour workshop kit for the support team at Horizonte Distribuidora, where Juliana works: diagnosis, script, exercises with answer key, incident mini-lesson, rubric, and follow-up. You rehearse, record 10 minutes, and evaluate yourself. At the end, you get the dashboard of what changed in the market in October 2026.

Who it's for

  1. 1People who will run AI workshops or bootcamps for company teams and leaders.
  2. 2Managers and HR, T&D or operations professionals who became the in-house AI reference.
  3. 3Consultants who want to add on-the-job training, with a method and no miracle promises.

What you'll be able to do

  1. 1Plan a lesson using 5 principles of adult learning.
  2. 2Diagnose the class and build a 1-hour, 4-hour or bootcamp workshop.
  3. 3Train the habit of delegating, reviewing and approving, adapted to each learner's area.
  4. 4Teach safety with real cases and assess the class with a 4-level rubric.
