Lesson 5 · 18 min

Adapt to the learner's area: sales, customer service, HR, legal, finance and operations

A salesperson doesn't want a lawyer's example, and finance doesn't want a marketing example. The same cycle of delegate, review and approve gets stronger when it uses the task, the vocabulary and the risk of each area. This lesson gives you the adaptation method and a map of INEMA's open courses by area, so you can build the track for each class.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The 4 ingredients of an adaptation
  2. 2Which INEMA open course fits each area
  3. 3The typical risk of each area to use in class
  4. 4A track template to copy

Goal: build a track for 1 area with tasks, vocabulary, typical risk and INEMA open courses with links.

01 · The 4 ingredients of the adaptation

From the learner's area to their track. Click each step.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Task and vocabulary hold attention; the typical risk becomes the approval exercise; the courses keep the study going afterward.

02 · Map: area → INEMA open courses

All free. Click the area to see tasks, risk and courses.

1. Sales

Tasks: preparing a proposal, summarizing a visit, answering a quote request, prioritizing the account list. Typical risk: a discount or deadline outside policy (lab failure F5).

Courses: 8020 — Sales, management and sales strategy · GPT-6 Astra in practice (delegate the whole proposal, with proof).

ExerciseA proposal for a new corner store, using the price table, with no discount above 5% without approval.

2. Customer service

Tasks: answering a complaint, notifying a delay, logging an exchange. Typical risk: an order hidden in the customer's message and personal data (failures F6 and F8).

Courses: Agente sob controle · criaagentes: a WhatsApp agent that never makes things up.

ExerciseReview 3 replies prepared by the AI for delay complaints, with planted errors.

3. HR and people

Tasks: job description, interview script, preparing a difficult conversation, development plan. Typical risk: an employee's personal data, or a decision about a person, handed over to AI.

Courses: Second Opinion (difficult conversations and developing people) · Companies of 2027 (people, processes and agents).

RuleAI helps prepare the conversation; the manager decides about the person.

4. Legal

Tasks: first read of a contract, deadlines, case summary. Typical risk: professional secrecy and sealed cases ending up in the wrong tool.

Course: Agents in the law firm (the agent reads, counts and prepares; the lawyer decides and signs).

ExerciseList the penalty clauses of a fictional contract and check each one against the original text.

5. Finance

Tasks: reconcile a bank statement, collect from a late-paying customer, explain the month's variance. Typical risk: the person who enters also approves, and customer data sent outside without a contract (failure F10).

Courses: Agents in accounting and finance · LOOP-R (improvement with numbers before and after).

RuleThe agent prepares the payment; a different person approves.

6. Operations and leadership

Tasks: map a process, set up an order routine, measure before and after, decide with clarity. Typical risk: automating without a baseline and not knowing whether it helped (failure F11).

Courses: AI Work Architect · Copilot + Agents for Companies · Agent Management: the 7 principles.

For everyoneA foundation for any area: From chat to agent and the Professional 2030 common base.

04 · Practice

A real area, with links you opened.

1 · Learning path for 1 area

30 min

Choose an area (from a real class or from Horizonte: sales with Patrícia, customer service with Juliana). Fill in the learning path with 3 tasks, the vocabulary, the typical risk and 2 or 3 courses from the map. Open each link before putting it in the learning path.

Done when: the learning path is saved, every link opens, and the typical risk has the name of who approves.

2 · Adapted exercise

15 min

Use the prompt to adapt exercise 2 from lesson 4 (Review) to the area you chose. Check the answer key the AI returned: it also goes through the 4 questions.

Done when: you have 1 exercise for the area with 3 planted errors and an answer key that you checked yourself.
