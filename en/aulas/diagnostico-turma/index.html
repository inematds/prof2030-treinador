Lesson 2 · 17 min

AI literacy as a product: diagnose the class and propose the training

Training that works for everyone works for no one. Before you design the workshop, find out what level the class is at, who already uses AI and who is afraid of it. With that in hand, your proposal to the owner stops being "an AI course" and becomes AI literacy with a goal and a measure.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The 4 levels of AI use in a team
  2. 2The 3 groups of people and the order of attention
  3. 3Why AI literacy became a requirement and a selling point
  4. 4Questionnaire and proposal ready to copy

Goal: give a diagnostic questionnaire, sort the class into 4 levels and write the training proposal on 1 page.

01 · The 4 levels of AI use

The class is almost never at just one level. Click to see what each level needs.

The 3 groups of people (active, curious, resistant) cut across the levels: there are resistant people at level 2 and curious people at level 0.

Design the workshop to take most people up one level, not to the highest level in the room.

02 · From diagnosis to proposal

Four steps, in the week before the workshop.

1. Give the questionnaire

Send the questionnaire a week ahead, through an anonymous form. Anonymous gets honest answers about fear and about tools people use on their own.

Ask the manager to say what it's for: designing the class, not evaluating anyone.

InvitationIt's 10 questions, 5 minutes, no name. It helps the class start from where you are.

2. Classify

Count how many people are at each level and split them into three groups: active (already use it and like it), curious (want to, but don't know where to start) and resistant (distrustful or afraid).

Active ones become allies and examples. Curious ones are the target of the workshop. Resistant ones won't be convinced in month 1: give them space and show them their colleagues' results.

Reading8 people: 2 at level 0, 4 at level 1, 2 at level 2. Target: move the 4 at level 1 up to level 2.

3. Find the shadow AI

One question in the questionnaire reveals tools the team uses without IT knowing. This is not to punish: it's a map of risk and of real interest.

At Horizonte, the inventory found 4 tools like this (lab failure F12).

QuestionWhat AI tools have you already used at work, even on your own?

4. Write the proposal

The proposal states the goal as a behavior ("answer a complaint with AI and review"), the format, what the team leaves able to do, and how we will measure in 30 days.

No promise of gains you haven't measured. State today's number and agree to measure again.

GoalIn 30 days, 6 of the 8 people in customer service use the response template with review in at least 10 cases per week.

03 · Why AI literacy became an argument

In the European Union, Article 4 of the AI Act requires those who provide or use AI systems to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy among their staff. This rule has applied since 02/02/2025, even with the postponement of the high-risk rules to 02/12/2027.

In Brazil there is no equivalent obligation in force, but the pressure is the same: Deloitte (State of AI in the Enterprise 2026) shows that only 27% of Brazilian companies say they have mature AI governance. Training the team is the first step of that governance, and it is an honest argument for the owner.

04 · Templates to copy

Questionnaire for the class and proposal for the person who hires you.

Class diagnostic questionnaire (anonymous)
CLASS DIAGNOSTIC · <empresa> · <área> · 5 minutes, no name

1. How often do you use AI at work?
   ( ) never ( ) I've tried it ( ) every week ( ) every day
2. For what? (check all that apply)
   ( ) text/email ( ) summary ( ) spreadsheet/analysis ( ) customer service ( ) other: ___
3. What AI tools have you already used at work, even on your own? ___
4. Have you ever handed a whole task to AI and reviewed it afterward? ( ) yes ( ) no
5. Before using an AI answer, what do you check? ___
6. Have you ever pasted customer data (name, CPF, phone number) into an AI? ( ) yes ( ) no ( ) I don't know
7. From 1 to 5, how much do you trust AI answers? ___
8. From 1 to 5, how worried are you about AI changing your job? ___
9. Which task of your week would you most like to do faster? ___
10. What would make this class worth it for you? ___

READING (for the trainer)
- Level 0: Q1 never/tried and Q4 no
- Level 1: Q1 week/day and Q4 no
- Level 2: Q4 yes and Q5 with a clear criterion
- Level 3: uses an agent that acts on its own (Q2/Q3)
- Groups: Q7 and Q8 high + high use = active; low use + clear Q10 = curious; Q8 high + Q7 low = resistant
- Q3 and Q6 = risk map (shadow AI, personal data)

05 · Practice

Use a real class or the Horizonte customer service team.

1 · Diagnostic ready

20 min

Copy the questionnaire, replace and <área> and adjust question 9 with real tasks from the area. If you have a class, run it. If not, answer as if you were 3 different people at Horizonte (Juliana, a new attendant and a skeptical attendant).

Done when: the questionnaire is ready to send and you classified at least 3 answers by level and group, with the reasoning for each.

2 · 1-page proposal

20 min

Fill in the proposal with the result of the diagnostic. The goal has to be a behavior someone can count in 30 days.

Done when: the proposal fits on 1 page, has a goal as a behavior, today's number and what is not included.
