1-hour workshop, 4-hour workshop and bootcamp: the same 5-part structure

The format changes, the backbone doesn't. A 1-hour workshop, a 4-hour morning and a 5-session bootcamp follow the same 5 parts: destination, route, flight plan, in-flight service and landing. In this lesson you build the 1-hour script and practice the hardest part: the skeptical participant.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The 5 parts of any workshop
  2. 2How the same backbone becomes 1 h, 4 h or a bootcamp
  3. 3Room rules: silence, leftovers and hands in pockets
  4. 4Guiding a skeptical participant in a simulation

Goal: build the full script for a 1-hour workshop in the 5 parts and know how to stretch it to 4 hours or to a 5-session bootcamp.

01 · The 5 parts

It works for 1 hour and for 5 sessions. Click each part.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

The destination and the landing are the parts the class remembers. If you run short on time, cut from the middle, never from the landing.

02 · Three formats, the same backbone

Choose by the goal, not by the client's open calendar.

1. 1 hour

Good for easing the fear, showing a use that works, and collecting 1 commitment. It doesn't build a habit on its own.

One real task, two practice rounds, a scheduled follow-up. Class of up to 12 people so there's time to walk the room.

DestinationLeave able to answer 1 type of complaint with AI and review.

2. 4 hours

The 5 parts with 3 real tasks from the area instead of 1, a 15-minute break, and a security block with a real case.

It ends with each person showing 1 result to their colleagues: public recognition teaches that using it is the expected thing.

Structure0:00 opening · 0:20 task 1 · 1:10 task 2 · 1:50 break · 2:05 security · 2:35 task 3 · 3:30 showcase and commitment · 3:50 landing.

3. Bootcamp

Five sessions of 1 to 2 hours, one per week. Each follows the 5 parts and opens with retrieval practice from the previous week.

Sequence: 1 delegate and review · 2 tasks from the area · 3 security · 4 personal project · 5 hands-on test and choosing the champions.

Why space them outA week between sessions gives people time to use it at work and come back with real cases.

4. Room rules

Prepare extra content: two spare blocks in case the room goes quiet, and one block you skip if they talk a lot.

Ask the question and wait 15 seconds. During practice, hands in your pockets: whoever touches the learner's mouse teaches them to watch, not to do.

AudienceAsk the client up front: is the class required to attend or there voluntarily? Required: open with a demonstration. Voluntary: they want the grounding.

03 · Simulated workshop: the skeptical participant

Simulated conversation for practice. A 1-hour workshop with the Horizonte customer service team. Choose what you would say.

    04 · Templates to copy

    The 1-hour script is the centerpiece of your kit (class 8).

    1-hour workshop script (5 parts)
    WORKSHOP · <topic> · <company> · <date>
Audience: <roles> · mandatory or voluntary: <which> · up to <n> people
DESTINATION (1 sentence): after this hour, you will be able to <do what>.

00-05 ROUTE
- Problem with a company number: <...>
- What the room thinks today: <...>
- Fear to name: <...>
- Flight plan: "around the 20-minute mark comes the densest part"

05-20 IN-FLIGHT SERVICE 1 · short demo + round 1
- Real task: <...>
- Question for the room (wait 15 s): <...>

20-40 IN-FLIGHT SERVICE 2 · the dense stretch + round 2
- How to review: <3-item checklist>
- Redo the task with the feedback from round 1

40-52 IN-FLIGHT SERVICE 3 · applied
- Risk case from the area (2 min): <...>
- Whoever found an error shows it to the room

52-60 LANDING
- 3 questions without looking at the material
- Commitment: 1 task, 10 cases, by <day>
- Follow-up: <date> · 15 min

BACKUP (silent room): <block A> / <block B>
SKIP (talkative room): <block>
    Prompt · prepare answers to objections
    I'm going to run a <duration> AI workshop for <area and roles> at <type of company>.
The diagnosis showed: <levels and groups>.

List the 5 most likely objections from this class (fear, distrust, lack of time, security, "I already tried").
For each one:
1. the sentence as the person would say it;
2. a short, honest answer, without promising what I don't control
   (jobs, guaranteed results, "AI doesn't make mistakes");
3. how to turn the objection into a 3-minute exercise.

    40 min

    Done when: all fields are filled in, the blocks add up to 60 minutes, there are 2 practice rounds, and a commitment with a date.

    2 · Simulated workshop

    10 min

    Done when: you finished with the top score and wrote, for 3 objections from your class, the answer you would give.

    06 · Quick quiz

    4 questions. The explanation appears after your attempt.

    Question 1 of 4

    The workshop ran 10 minutes late. What do you cut?

    Question 2 of 4

    The client wants "a 1-hour workshop so the team becomes AI experts." What do you say?

    Question 3 of 4

    You asked a question and the room went silent for 5 seconds. What do you do?

    Question 4 of 4

    A participant asks whether AI will cut jobs at the company. Which answer is the most honest?

    07 · Summary

    5 partsDestination, route, flight plan, in-flight service, and landing.
    3 formats1 h opens the door, 4 h trains 3 tasks, a bootcamp builds habit over 5 sessions.
    Room rulesSpare prepared, 15 seconds of silence, hands in pockets.
    The skeptic is materialValidate, measure, acknowledge the finding, and don't promise what you don't control.
