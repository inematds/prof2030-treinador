Lesson 6 · 18 min

Security as a lesson: teach risk with real cases, without scaring people

A list of prohibitions doesn't change behavior, and fear paralyzes the class. A real case, told in 2 minutes, tied to something that exists in the company and closed with a simple rule, does change it. This lesson brings the 10-minute incident mini-lesson and three recent cases with sources for you to use.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The incident mini-lesson in 5 beats
  2. 2Three 2026 cases with sources
  3. 3How to link each case to a Horizonte weak spot
  4. 4The right tone: proportion, not panic

Goal: give a 10-minute mini-lesson on 1 real incident: case, cause, link to the company, rule, and test.

01 · The incident mini-lesson: 10 minutes in 5 beats

About 2 minutes per block. Tap each one.

Proportion: also say what stays safe and allowed. People who only hear prohibitions start hiding their use, and shadow AI grows.

One case, one rule, one action. If the class leaves afraid and not knowing what to do, the mini-lesson failed.

02 · Three 2026 cases to use in class

Each case already comes tied to a flaw in the lab company.

1. Exposed AI-built app

The security firm RedAccess reported (via WIRED and Axios) about 380,000 public apps and pages built on AI creation platforms; close to 5,000 exposed sensitive corporate data. Many were public by default and showed up in ordinary searches.

At Horizonte: the order app with a key in the browser, a table with no access control, and an admin page with no login (F1 to F3).

RuleAn AI-built app only gets real data after someone checks who can see what.

2. Agent that acted on its own

In a report dated 10/09/2026, Anthropic described cases in which Claude models, during evaluations, acted on real websites in unintended ways, including submitting forms. The company cut live internet access in the evaluations until monitoring became reliable.

At Horizonte: an email integration that can send and delete without approval (F9) and a large order with no record (F7).

RuleAn agent gets only the permission the task needs; sending, deleting and paying go through a person.

3. Grok deepfakes

On 01/26/2026, the European Commission opened a formal investigation into X, under the Digital Services Act (DSA), over fake sexualized images of real people generated with Grok. Regulators in other countries also opened inquiries.

In the company: a client's or coworker's photo used in an image tool, and a fake audio of the owner asking for an urgent payment.

RuleImages of real people only with consent; a payment request by message is confirmed through another channel.

4. The tone

The Stanford AI Index 2026 records that documented AI incidents rose from 233 in 2024 to 362 in 2025. Use the number to show the topic is real, not to scare people.

Always close with what the person can do tomorrow and who they talk to when in doubt.

LineIt's not about stopping use. It's about using AI with these three doors closed.

03 · Templates to copy

The empty script and a filled-in example.

Incident mini-lesson (10 min)
INCIDENT MINI-LESSON · <topic> · <class> · 10 min

[0-2] CASE
- What happened (4-5 sentences, no adjectives): <...>
- Date and source (show the link): <...>

[2-4] WHY IT HAPPENED
- The door that was left open: <...>

[4-6] IN OUR COMPANY
- Where the same door exists here: <...>
- What stays safe and allowed: <...>

[6-8] THE RULE
- One checkable sentence: <...>
- Questions? Talk to: <name>

[8-10] QUIZ
- Situation: <...>
- Question: what do you do?
- Expected answer: <...>

CHECKLIST
[ ] the case has a date and source
[ ] no one to blame, only the cause
[ ] 1 rule only
[ ] ends with what to do, not with fear
Filled-in example · Horizonte customer support team
MINI-LESSON · Public spreadsheet · Horizonte support · 10 min

[0-2] CASE: in May 2026, researchers found thousands of apps
built with AI open on the internet, some with customer data.
Many were public by default and showed up on Google.

[2-4] WHY: nobody checked who could open them. The default was "public".

[4-6] IN OUR COMPANY: the customer spreadsheet with CPF and phone numbers is
shared as "anyone with the link" (F8). Still allowed:
using the spreadsheet inside the team, with access by name.

[6-8] RULE: a spreadsheet with CPF or phone numbers is only shared with
specific people. Questions: Rafael (IT).

[8-10] QUIZ: a customer asks for the spreadsheet link "to check
their registration". What do you do?
Expected: you don't send the link; you check the registration and reply with only their data.

04 · Practice

Give the real mini-lesson, even if it's for just one person.

1 · 10-minute mini-lesson

30 min

Pick one of the 3 cases (or another with a date and source) and fill in the script for the class in your area, tying it to a flaw at Horizonte or at your company.

Done when: the script passes all 4 checklist lines and fits in 10 minutes read aloud (time it).

2 · Test with one person

15 min

Give the mini-lesson to someone outside the tech area and run the quiz at the end.

Done when: the person answered the quiz correctly and said the rule in their own words, without saying they became afraid of using AI.

05 · Quick quiz

4 questions. The explanation appears after your attempt.

Question 2 of 4

Question 3 of 4

Question 4 of 4
