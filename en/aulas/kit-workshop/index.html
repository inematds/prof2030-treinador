Lesson 8 · 25 min

Workshop kit for Horizonte and what changed in the market

Time to put the pieces together. The final project is a 1-hour workshop kit for the support team at Horizonte Distribuidora, where Juliana works: diagnosis, script, exercises with answer key, incident mini-lesson, rubric, and follow-up. You rehearse, record 10 minutes, and evaluate yourself. At the end, you get the dashboard of what changed in the market in October 2026.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  3. 3How to ask for a second opinion on the kit
  4. 4What changed in the market and affects this program

Goal: deliver the complete 1-hour workshop kit for Horizonte's support team and give a recorded 10-minute lesson, evaluated with the rubric.

02 · From kit to scored lesson

Four steps, in this order.

1. Assemble

Put the pieces from lessons 1 to 7 into one folder: diagnostico, roteiro, exercicios, incidente, rubrica, acompanhamento. Adapt everything to Horizonte's support work, using the vocabulary of the field.

Use only fictional lab data. No CPF or phone number, not even made-up ones that look real.

Workshop destinationAfter this hour, the team answers complaints with AI, reviews with the 4 questions, and knows what only goes out with approval from someone with a name.

2. Rehearse and record

Pick the hardest stretch: the opening with the fear named, or the review exercise. Record with your phone, speaking as you would to the class.

If you can, ask someone to play the skeptical participant, like Sérgio in lesson 3.

TipTime it. If the 10-minute stretch ran past 13, the 1-hour script won't fit.

3. Assess

Watch the recording and score yourself on the 5 criteria in the checklist below, from 1 to 4. Note 1 thing to keep and 1 to change.

Ask AI for a second opinion with the ready-made prompt. It doesn't decide for you: it points out what you may be overlooking.

Useful feedbackKeep: the discount case. Change: I waited only 4 seconds after the question.

4. Adjust and deliver

Fix the script with what the recording showed and complete the kit checklist.

In real life, show the kit to the client (here, Carlos, the owner, with Juliana) before the workshop and agree on the number that will be measured in 30 days.

DeliverableFolder with 6 files + link to the 10-minute video + completed checklist.

03 · Templates to copy

The checklist closes the kit; the prompt gives the second opinion.

Kit checklist + AI trainer rubric
KIT CHECKLIST · 1-hour workshop · customer service · Horizonte Distribuidora

PARTS
[ ] Diagnostic: questionnaire + reading (levels, groups, shadow AI)
[ ] 1-hour script in 5 parts, adding up to 60 min, with a buffer and a block to skip
[ ] The 5 principles marked in the script
[ ] 3 exercises (delegate, review, approve) with answer key, customer service cases
[ ] Incident mini-lesson (2-minute version inside the hour) with date and source
[ ] 4-level rubric adapted to customer service
[ ] Follow-up plan: champion + backup, weekly check-in, 30/60/90
[ ] Today's number agreed on to measure in 30 days
[ ] Fictional data only; no promise of jobs or guaranteed results

AI TRAINER RUBRIC (10-min recording · 1 to 4 each)
1. Why: did you open with the class's problem, with a number?   ___
2. Practice: does the class do more than watch?                  ___
3. Retrieval: did you ask and wait ~15 s?                       ___
4. Safety: risk with a real case, without alarmism?             ___
5. Landing: commitment with a date and a follow-up scheduled?   ___
Keep: <...>   Change: <...>
Prompt · second opinion on the kit
I'm going to give a 1-hour AI workshop for the customer service team of a
cleaning products distributor (fictional company, 40 people).
Here are my script and exercises:
"""<paste the script and the exercises>"""

Do not rewrite the material. Help me think through:
1. the real situation: what this script promises to deliver in 1 hour;
2. the options I have for the 2 weakest parts;
3. the risk of each option (time, skeptical class, safety);
4. what I might be overlooking about this class;
5. what to validate with the manager before giving the lesson.
Point out any promise I can't keep.

07 · What changed (Oct/2026)

Dashboard updated every quarter. The method in lessons 1 to 7 stays; the facts below change.

AI literacy required in the EU (since 02/02/2025). Article 4 of the AI Act requires AI literacy measures for people who use AI at work. It is the most direct argument for selling training. Article 4.

High risk postponed to 02/12/2027 (agreement of 05/07/2026). The Omnibus package postponed the high-risk rules in Annex III; AI literacy still applies. Gibson Dunn.

Agents that deliver finished work (07/09/2026). ChatGPT Work produces documents, spreadsheets, and slides, and works for hours. That is why lesson 4 trains you to delegate, review and approve, not to write prompts. ChannelLife.

Exposed AI-built apps (May/2026). About 380 thousand public apps were scanned, and close to 5 thousand held sensitive company data. A ready-made case for lesson 6. HKU.

Agent that acted without authorization (10/09/2026). Anthropic reported models acting on real websites during evaluations, including submitting forms, and cut off live internet access in those tests. ResultSense.

Grok deepfakes under investigation (01/26/2026). The European Commission opened a formal investigation into X under the Digital Services Act. A case for talking about images of real people and voice scams. Al Jazeera.

Brazil: governance trailing adoption (Sep/2026). Only 27% of Brazilian companies say they have mature AI governance, according to Deloitte. Training the team is the first step. Deloitte.
